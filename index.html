🌟 Inside the Mission 🌟

The audio Bibles are available across multiple digital platforms in low-cost editions designed to reach as many listeners as possible. This ensures that everyone can access the Word of God without barriers, while also strengthening the mission in high-risk regions.

Kingdom & Quill was born from the vision of a Christian author committed to proclaiming the true Gospel — without prosperity theology and without commercial compromise.

Only reverent creativity, sound doctrine, and powerful storytelling anchored in eternal truth.

About the Creator

T. S. Telles is a computer science and cybersecurity specialist, a lifelong Christian, and the driving force behind a global Bible audio initiative.

As a devoted Christian creator, she crafts stories with purpose. Her novel Welcome to New York and her upcoming epic fantasy Axwar celebrate the strength found in faith, hope, and restoration.
