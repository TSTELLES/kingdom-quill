What You’ll Find Here

The fiction titles presented by Kingdom & Quill are written by T. S. Telles and crafted to stand shoulder-to-shoulder with mainstream entertainment in the romance and fantasy genres. Though designed to entertain, these stories carry themes that naturally resonate with the Christian community and, out of respect for the convictions behind this project, they avoid explicit sexual content and any values that clash with a life of faith. You won’t find identity-driven manifestos or crude language here. What you’ll find are deep narratives—plots that balance tension and purpose without losing their edge.

If you came for romance, suspense, or fantasy, you’re in the right place. Check each book’s content rating and its availability in your preferred language.

Sit back. Explore. And let each story speak for itself. God bless you.

Welcome to New York

Part 1: Born Ricciwood

Genre: Contemporary Romance, Christian Romance, Urban Romance, Emotional Drama
Language: English (US), Portuguese (Brazil)

Gabriel Ricciwood is haunted by what he’s done—and what he’s lost. Shattered by a broken relationship and consumed by the guilt and rage left in the wake of his mother’s death, he walks the razor’s edge between right and wrong.

When he earns a scholarship to an elite school in New York City, Gabriel finds himself pulled into a legacy long buried by the city’s powerful council. There, he meets Eduarda—a girl whose face awakens something buried deep within him, and whose past is more connected to his own than either of them is prepared to face.

With the return of the final heir to New York’s forgotten sixth founding family, old secrets rise to the surface, and the past comes calling. But can Gabriel rewrite a fate written in blood—or will he become exactly what he’s been running from?

Lost Gospel

When the Church Stands in the Way of Christ

Genre: Nonfiction / Religious, Christian Criticism, Theological Essay, Religious Commentary
Language: English (US), Portuguese (Brazil), Spanish

Lost Gospel: When the Church Stands in the Way of Christ is not a comfortable book — and it never intended to be. It gathers real stories and direct reflections on wounds caused not by the world, but from within the church itself.

People sincere in their faith have been silenced, manipulated, or ignored by structures more concerned with appearance, control, and status than with the truth of the gospel. Here, Christ’s name becomes a brand or shield; the Word is replaced by human conventions; grace is reduced to a confrontation-free speech.

With solid biblical grounding, the book exposes false gospels and normalized practices that push people away from God. It doesn’t attack Christian faith — it defends it.

O Poder dos Quatro Tronos

Genre: Fantasy, Adventure, Fiction
Language: Português (BR)

Assim como o poder dos deuses, há muito tempo o tecido que unia os bruxos e as criaturas mágicas foi corrompido. Um reino que antes vivia em harmonia mergulhou na escuridão, dividido pela obsessão de um rei.

Durante uma era inteira, um homem perverso governou o Grande Norte com mentiras e falsas narrativas. Bruxos com poderes incompreendidos foram escravizados e punidos por usar sua magia — não para dominar, mas para sobreviver.

Neste primeiro livro da saga Axwar, Alyce é despertada por um chamado antigo: ela carrega um poder destinado a restaurar a justiça e reinar com sabedoria sobre os oprimidos. Mas num mundo atemporal, repleto de bruxos, dragões, caçadores, vampiros e criaturas sombrias, sua maior batalha não será contra monstros… e sim contra os rostos familiares que escondem traições.

Para herdar o trono, Alyce precisará distinguir aliados de inimigos — antes que seja tarde demais.
