# Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 6, 2026

Driver Stats is developed by T S Telles.

This Privacy Policy explains how Driver Stats collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use the app.

## Information We Collect

Driver Stats may collect and store information you provide in the app, including:

- Account information, such as email address, display name, and user ID.
- Vehicle information, such as vehicle name, brand, model, year, fuel type, consumption, and maintenance cost.
- Trip information, such as trip amount, distance, time, estimated costs, and estimated profit.
- Cost records, such as fuel, charging, maintenance, and other vehicle-related expenses.
- App settings, such as selected language, country, currency, appearance preferences, and plan status.
- Plan and usage information, such as Free plan limits, trial status, premium status, calculation count, and billing product identifiers.

## How We Use Information

We use this information to:

- Provide trip profitability estimates.
- Save vehicles, trips, costs, rates, and settings.
- Sync account data through Firebase.
- Apply Free, trial, and premium plan rules.
- Improve app functionality and reliability.
- Help users estimate driving income, costs, and profitability.

Driver Stats provides estimates only. Actual income, fuel use, energy use, maintenance, taxes, platform fees, traffic, and vehicle costs may vary.

## Third-Party Services

Driver Stats may use third-party services, including:

- Firebase Authentication, for account login.
- Cloud Firestore, for storing user app data.
- Firebase services, for app functionality and synchronization.
- Google Play Billing, if paid plans are enabled in a future version.
- Google AdMob, if ads or rewarded ads are enabled.

These services may process data according to their own privacy policies and security practices.

## Advertising

Future versions of Driver Stats may show ads through Google AdMob or similar advertising services. Rewarded ads may allow users to receive an in-app benefit, such as an additional calculation, after choosing to watch an ad.

## Data Sharing

Driver Stats does not sell user data.

User data may be processed by service providers necessary for app functionality, such as Firebase, Google Play, and AdMob.

## Data Security

We use reasonable security measures to protect user data. Data transmitted between the app and Firebase services is protected using standard security mechanisms provided by those services.

No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.
